LBefore streaming, television was strictly broadcasted over the airwaves. You could purchase a physical copy of a show or movie, and if you had access to an internet connection, there were services to download movies and TV shows, but this usually would take hours, even sometimes up to a day depending on the file size and your connection’s bandwidth. Otherwise, you were at the will of the broadcast, where the shows you’d watch were transmitted to your home through radio signals: video was transmitted over AM and audio was FM. (Silva) Streaming, however, is done entirely over the internet. Instead of downloading an entire file onto your device and playing it locally, streaming lets video play in a browser. Like everything on the internet, audio and video data is broken down into smaller chunks, called packets, which are encoded and compressed before the data leaves the server, and then decoded once it reaches the user’s device. (Rogers) YouTube also uses HTTP-based adaptive bitrate streaming which transcodes video data to adapt it for different device limitations and internet strength so that users can get the highest quality content possible in the shortest time. Buffering is also a tool used to help load videos across different bandwidths and internet speeds, where the media player loads the video a few seconds ahead.
